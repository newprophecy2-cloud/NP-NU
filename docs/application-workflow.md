# NP-NU Application Preparation & Handoff Workflow

## 1. Boundary Between NP-NU and Official NU Portal

NP-NU provides guided application **preparation, validation, preference planning, and student-owned progress tracking**. Official submission, payment (Sonali Seba / mobile banking), and admission confirmation happen exclusively on National University's official portals (`http://app.nu.edu.bd/nu-web/applicantLogin.action` / `http://app1.nu.edu.bd/`).

## 2. Guided Multi-Step Draft Builder (`/dashboard/applications/new` & `/dashboard/applications/[id]`)

1. **Step 1 — Programme & Academic Session:** Choose active NU programme (e.g. Honours, Degree Pass, Masters Professional, PGD) and verified session; view circular deadline & status.
2. **Step 2 — Student & Contact Information:** Student display name and optional contact details (no NID or NU portal password collected).
3. **Step 3 — SSC Academic Record:** Board, Roll, Registration (optional), Passing Year, Group, GPA (`1.00–5.00`).
4. **Step 4 — HSC Academic Record:** Board, Roll, Registration (optional), Passing Year, Group, GPA (`1.00–5.00`).
5. **Step 5 — Eligibility Verification:** Evaluates draft against the circular's verified `eligibility_rules` (`ELIGIBLE`, `NOT_ELIGIBLE`, `INSUFFICIENT_INFO`, or `MANUAL_VERIFICATION_REQUIRED`) with exact rule citation.
6. **Step 6 — College & Subject Preferences:** Search and rank verified affiliated colleges and subjects.
7. **Step 7 — Review & Print Summary:** Full validation check, missing-field report, and printable preparation summary.
8. **Step 8 — Official Portal Handoff & Tracking:** Explicit disclaimer, official circular PDF link, external portal launch button (`rel="noopener noreferrer"`), and student-reported tracking states (`opened_official_portal`, `submitted_by_student`, `payment_pending`, `payment_completed`, `confirmation_recorded`, etc.).
